Last updated: 13 October 2026

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy and meet legal obligations. We then delete or de-identify it where appropriate.

You can contact us to request access to or correction of your personal information, request deletion, or raise a privacy concern. Some information may need to be retained for legal or transaction records.

We will review your request and respond in accordance with applicable law.

Orders

All orders are subject to product availability and payment confirmation. We confirm acceptance of your order after verifying payment and availability.

Please check your selected items, sizes, quantities and delivery details before completing your purchase.

If we cannot fulfil an order because an item is unavailable, payment cannot be verified or there is a material pricing error, we will contact you. Any payment received for cancelled items will be refunded.

If you need to change or cancel an order, contact us as soon as possible. We will try to assist, but changes or cancellations may not be possible once your order has been dispatched.

Returns & Exchanges

Last updated: 13 October 2026

We want you to feel comfortable in your ISALONE pieces. Please check our size guide and product descriptions before purchasing.

Size exchanges

You may request a size exchange within 7 days of receiving your order.

Items must be unworn, unwashed and in their original condition, with all original tags attached. Trying an item on to check the fit is fine.

Exchanges are subject to stock availability. If your requested size is unavailable, you may choose another available item or receive store credit for the returned item’s purchase price. Any price difference will be confirmed before proceeding.

Customers are responsible for return shipping and delivery costs for size exchanges. These costs will be confirmed before you proceed. Original shipping fees are not refundable for size exchanges.

Change of mind

We do not offer refunds for change of mind, including a change in preference after purchasing. Size exchanges are available under the conditions above.

This does not affect any rights you have under applicable consumer law.

Faulty, damaged or incorrect items

If you receive a faulty, damaged or incorrect item, contact us as soon as possible with your order number, a description of the issue and, where possible, photos.

We will assess the issue and arrange an appropriate replacement, refund or other remedy under applicable consumer law. ISALONE will cover reasonable return and replacement delivery costs where the issue is our responsibility.

The 7-day size exchange deadline and original-condition requirements do not limit your rights regarding faulty, damaged or incorrect items.

How to request a return or exchange

Email isalone.studios@gmail.com or message @isalone_studio on Instagram with your order number and request.

Please contact us before sending items back so we can provide the correct return address and instructions. For an approved size exchange, please send the item back within 7 days of receiving our return instructions.

Refunds

Where a refund is approved, we will arrange it through the original payment method where possible, or another method agreed with you.

Approved refunds will be issued within 7 business days. Your bank or payment provider may take additional time to complete the transaction.

International orders

For specially arranged overseas orders, additional exchange shipping costs and return arrangements will be explained before payment.

This does not limit your rights under applicable consumer law.
